About

A bit about me

I'm a high school student with a strong interest in engineering, especially where hardware and software come together. Whether I'm designing parts in CAD, soldering together a custom embedded system, or building a tool that makes my life easier, I enjoy creating things that are both functional and well thought out.

I serve as the Mechanical Lead on my FTC robotics team, where I oversee the design, prototyping, and manufacturing of our competition robots. This past season, we set the solo world record and global match world record at a competition back in November, and the autonomous world record at the NorCal regional championship.

I hold a CSWA certification in SolidWorks. Outside of robotics, I work on personal projects to solve real problems, explore new ideas, or simply satisfy a creative urge.
