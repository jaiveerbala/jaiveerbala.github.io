About

A bit about me

I'm a high school student with a deep interest in engineering across the full stack — mechanical, electrical, and software. Whether I'm machining parts in CAD, soldering together a custom embedded system, or writing a web app that actually fits the way I work, I care about doing it well.

I serve as the Mechanical Lead on my FTC robotics team, where I oversee the design, prototyping, and manufacturing of competition robots. This past season we set the solo world record, the global match world record, and the autonomous world record at the NorCal regional championship.

I hold a CSWA certification in SolidWorks. Outside of robotics, I build personal projects that solve real problems — or just scratch a creative itch.
