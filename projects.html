Robotics · 2025–26
FTC Decode Robot
▶ sorting_vid
▶ 21_ball_auton

My FTC team's robot for the 2025-26 season features some of the most advanced mechanical concepts my team has ever implemented. As Mechanical Lead, I oversaw the design, production, and assembly of every subsystem while personally engineering the drivetrain and turreted shooter — a ball launcher mounted on an independent turret that rotates without the robot itself turning.

  • Set the solo world record and global match world record in November 2025
  • Set the autonomous world record at the NorCal Regional Championship
  • Personally designed the full drivetrain — uses an external reduction from 6000 to 460 RPM at the wheel via a dual-stage reduction system designed to save weight (lighter than the steel gearbox that ships with the motor)
  • Personally designed the turreted shooter mechanism for independent rotation from the robot
  • Personally designed the parking mechanism
  • Led design, production, and assembly of all other mechanisms as Mechanical Lead

Mechanical · 2025
Differential Swerve Drivetrain
▶ roll test
▶ pod test
▶ swerve test

Differential gearing is a common principle in robotics. I applied it to a drivetrain where two motors per pod, acting through a differential, can independently control both wheel rotation speed and pod steering angle simultaneously, giving infinite combinations of motion with just two motors per pod for four total. This is the second iteration of the project, featuring better motor placement, more compact pods, and overall faster driving.

  • Designed entirely in SolidWorks CAD from scratch — solo personal project
  • Manufactured, assembled, and tested the full physical prototype
  • Two pods, four motors total — omnidirectional capability
  • 3D-printed prototype validated the concept; used for testing purposes
Mechanical · 2025
2-Speed Transmission

A servo-actuated 2-speed transmission designed in SolidWorks for FTC robotics. Switches between 1:1 and 5:3 gear ratios in under a second using a spring-loaded linear shift along a shaft. Particularly useful for endgame tasks where a mechanism needs to be fast during normal play but requires more torque at the end of the match.

  • Designed in SolidWorks CAD
  • Switches between 1:1 and 5:3 torque ratio in under one second
  • Spring-loaded linear shift actuated by a servo motor
  • Designed for FTC endgame linear slide applications requiring extra torque
Mechanical · 2025
Differential Wrist
▶ sample transfer

A two-DOF wrist mechanism for the Into the Deep season robot. Uses a differential gearing system so that both the orbit angle and the claw rotation can be controlled simultaneously with cleaner wire routing and simpler kinematics than handling each DOF separately.

  • Designed in SolidWorks CAD
  • Differential gearing achieves simultaneous orbit and rotation of the claw
  • Enables picking up specimens off the field wall and repositioning for optimal scoring
  • Simpler wire management versus two independently driven DOFs

Embedded · Electrical · 2023 · 7th Grade
Acoustic Levitator
▶ 1 ball test
▶ levitator zoomed

Standing waves between two opposing ultrasonic transducers create pressure nodes that suspend small objects in midair without any physical contact. Built by desoldering the transmitters from SR04 sensors and driving them with a motor controller for amplification.

  • Desoldered ultrasonic transmitters from SR04 sensors
  • Powered through a motor controller to amplify output beyond stock driver circuits
  • Required careful physical alignment and distance calibration to find the standing wave sweet spot
  • 3D-printed enclosure once a working alignment was found
  • Plug-in power cable on the back powers both the Arduino and motor controller
  • Can stack multiple pieces of foam into spinning rings of levitated balls

Embedded · 2025 · 8th Grade
Raspberry Pi CAD File Server
▶ IMG_1762
▶ IMG_1763

Built a self-hosted file server for my FTC robotics team to solve a workflow problem: SolidWorks didn't offer free student PDM (file sharing) at the time, leaving the team without a reliable way to sync CAD files across members.

  • Raspberry Pi 3B+ running 24/7 as a persistent sync node
  • Resilio Sync monitors the shared folder and pushes changes to all connected devices
  • Always-on server ensures the latest files are available even when all other devices are offline
  • Automatically boots and reconnects after a power disconnect
  • Built as a workaround before SolidWorks offered free student PDM licenses

Unbeatable Tic-Tac-Toe
You are X. Your turn.
The original program was written to be played in the Python console. This is a web version built with the same underlying logic and algorithms, just with a nicer UI.
▶ computer win
▶ tie game

A Python console Tic-Tac-Toe game against the computer (code). No machine learning or AI, just hardcoded logic, multi-step simulated lookaheads, fork detection and blocking. The result is a computer that essentially can't be beaten; most games end in a draw.

  • Python console-based, player vs. computer
  • Computer attempts to fork the player (create two simultaneous winning threats)
  • Computer detects and blocks player fork attempts early
  • Uses hardcoded cases, search algorithms, and multi-step simulated outcome trees — no AI
  • Essentially unbeatable — most games result in a tie; some in a computer win

CleanFlight — AI Drone Cleaning Fleet
▶ Revolve render

A concept developed for an engineering challenge — a self-coordinating fleet of AI-powered drones that can autonomously clean large outdoor areas far faster than any human crew.

  • Fleet of drones with onboard AI and hive-mind coordination
  • Each drone equipped with a vacuum pickup system for debris collection
  • Central ground station for takeoff, landing, and trash deposit
  • Area divided into sectors — each drone assigned and responsible for its own sector
  • Battery sized to complete a full sector on a single charge
  • All drones equipped with computer vision and obstacle avoidance sensors
  • What would take human crews days takes this fleet minutes
